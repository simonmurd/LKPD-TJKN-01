2 Ringkasan Teori Professional: WLAN Site Survey & Capacity Planning

RF Engineering & Survey Standard

Metrik Kualitas Sinyal (RSSI & SNR)

  • Sangat Baik (≥ -65 dBm): Ideal untuk Voice/Video Streaming.
  • Baik (-66 s.d. -72 dBm): Layak untuk Web Browsing & Email.
  • Batas Minimum (-75 dBm): Amandemen handoff/roaming klien.
  • SNR Ideal (≥ 25 dB): Sinyal jauh di atas noise floor (-95 dBm).

Benchmark Redaman Material Bangunan

  • Kaca & Partition: Redaman ringan (~1–3 dB).
  • Dinding Gypsum: Redaman sedang (~3–5 dB).
  • Dinding Bata / Hebel: Redaman tinggi (~6–10 dB).
  • Beton & Logam: Redaman ekstrem (~15–25+ dB, Deadzone).

Metodologi Site Survey WLAN

  • Predictive Survey: Simulasi perangkat lunak (CAD & Attenuation Model).
  • Passive Survey: Scanning beacon RF tanpa terhubung ke AP.
  • Active Survey & Walktest: Pengujian throughput (iPerf), latency, & roaming.
